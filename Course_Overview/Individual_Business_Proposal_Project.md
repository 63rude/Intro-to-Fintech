# Individual Business Proposal Project

Source: `Projects/Business proposal/Business proposal project.pdf`

## Title

Business Case Project: Proposal on FinTech Application and Disruption

## Type

Individual project, assisted by Generative AI or other AI tools.

## Weight

20% of the overall course grade.

## Due Date

Due by the presentation day in week 9.

## Objective

Propose a specific business application, use case, or startup pitch related to financial technologies learned in class. Examples include blockchain applications or new digital financial products.

The business idea should originate from the student's own thinking and then be reinforced or improved using AI. The handout says innovative ideas are encouraged and that score punishment will apply if the idea was originally generated by AI.

## Required Submission

Submit both:

- A written business proposal report.
- A dialogue record between the student and the AI assistant.

## Formatting Requirements

- Double-spaced.
- Cover page required.
- Font size at least 11.
- APA citation format.
- Length: 3 to 5 pages, including graphs, figures, and tables.
- The same format requirements apply to both the proposal and the AI-human dialogue record.

## Grading Criteria

Points are assigned based on:

- Contribution.
- Novelty.
- Degree of completion.
- Quality of human-AI interaction.

## Expected Work

Students are expected to:

- Research the topic by reading papers or articles.
- Discuss and provide ideas on innovation, business model, and implementation.
- Ensure the proposed idea is not already widely used or observed as of 2026.
- Contribute to writing, data collection if needed, and coding if the idea depends on market statistics.
- Cover both business and technical aspects.

## Proposal Content Checklist

The proposal should cover:

- Problem being solved.
- Proposed solution.
- Technical design or implementation logic.
- Potential challenges.
- Competitors.
- Market and revenue sources.
- Summary statistics where relevant.
- Business design and solution quality.

## AI Interaction Requirement

Students must use Generative AI tools to improve the business proposal. Credit can be extended based on the depth and breadth of the recorded AI-human conversation.

Possible AI tools named in the handout:

- Deepseek
- ChatGPT
- Gemini
- Claude

## Suggested Resources

- Class notes and reading articles from the course.
- News sources such as Bloomberg Businessweek, Wall Street Journal, and Financial Times.
- Government policies or announcements.
