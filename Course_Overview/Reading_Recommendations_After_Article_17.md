## Final four recommendations

### 1. Article 16 - Robo-Advisors: Product vs. Platform

Read this first.

Main payoff:

- Product versus distribution/platform power.
- Trust in human versus automated service.
- Brand adoption and bundling inside broader digital ecosystems.
- A fintech case that generalizes to many consumer products.

Best assignment angle:

> When a service becomes digital, is the winning product the best expert tool, the cheapest substitute for a human, or the platform that controls distribution and trust?

### 2. Article 15 - The Challenger Banks That Threaten to Disrupt Financial Institutions

Main payoff:

- How incumbents expose themselves through culture, silos, infrastructure, and slow product improvement.
- Why consumer routines change before institutions admit the threat is serious.
- Partnerships versus internal transformation.
- Trust as an incumbent advantage that can erode.

Best assignment angle:

> Are challenger firms winning because of technology, or because incumbents organize products and customer experience badly?

### 3. Article 11 - The Digitalization of Payments and Currency: Some Issues for Consideration

Main payoff:

- Payments as infrastructure for everyday life.
- Network effects, BigTech, data, privacy, inclusion, and consumer protection.
- Why public institutions care about the medium and rails of exchange.
- A compact article with a strong institutional and social lens.

Best assignment angle:

> What changes socially and politically when payment systems move from regulated intermediaries toward data-rich platforms?

### 4. Article 1 - FinTech and market structure in financial services

Read this when you want the broadest systems piece.

Main payoff:

- How innovation changes market structure, not just features.
- The relationship among incumbents, startups, BigTech firms, regulators, and infrastructure providers.
- Customer expectations, market concentration, network effects, open banking, and dependence on third parties.
- Good raw material for asking AI questions that reach beyond finance.

Best assignment angle:

> Does digital innovation actually decentralize an industry, or can it simply replace old concentration with new platform concentration?

## Reading order

Recommended order for interest and variety:

1. Article 16 - product, platforms, trust.
2. Article 15 - management, incumbents, customer experience.
3. Article 11 - payments, society, governance.
4. Article 1 - broad system structure and regulation.

## Backup swap

If article 1 feels too report-like after the first few pages, swap in article 3.

Article 3 is the best backup because it is not mainly about financial technicalities. Its stronger parts are about how leaders evaluate AI adoption, data ethics, bias, regulation, hiring, and whether a technology use case has real business meaning beyond hype.
