# Reading Assignments

Source: `Readings/Reading Assignment.pdf` and the article files under `Readings/Readings/`.

## Assignment Type

The reading assignment is AI-assisted and recurring. Students are expected to read articles from the provided list every two weeks and use Generative AI tools as part of the learning process.

## Requirements

- Pick one article at the beginning of every two-week period.
- Prepare a summary and reading notes before the due date.
- Include your own discussion, extension, or critique of the article.
- Ask AI 5 in-depth questions based on the article.
- Learn from and collect the AI's answers.
- Submit the dialogue record between you and the AI.
- The dialogue record should clearly show:
  - Which article you studied.
  - The article summary.
  - The Q&A jointly prepared by you and AI.

## Grading

- Each completed article and associated task can earn up to 5% credit on the final grade.
- Credit can be discounted if there is no evidence of active learning.
- Scores are based on the quality of your questions.
- The instructions encourage counter-arguments, criticism, and correction of AI output.

## Submission Frequency

Submission is every two weeks.

## Suggested Submission Structure

1. Article identification: title, file name, and topic.
2. Summary: main claim, evidence, and conclusion.
3. Your own notes: why the article matters for FinTech.
4. Five AI questions and answers.
5. Critique: where the AI answer was strong, weak, incomplete, or wrong.
6. Extension: one implication for financial institutions, investors, regulators, or startups.
